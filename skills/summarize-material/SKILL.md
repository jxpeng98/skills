# Summarize Material

## Purpose

Compress source material into useful decisions, facts, and next actions without losing uncertainty.

## Workflow

1. Identify the source type and the user's likely use of the summary.
2. Preserve concrete facts, dates, names, decisions, risks, and action items.
3. Separate facts from interpretation when the source is ambiguous.
4. Do not invent missing context or smooth over contradictions.
5. Keep quotes short and only when wording matters.

## Output Options

Choose the most useful structure:

- Executive summary
- Decisions and rationale
- Action items with owners and dates
- Risks and open questions
- Theme synthesis across multiple sources

State when the source is incomplete or when conclusions are inferred.
