# Academic editing

## Editing for publication

Return a usable revision of the author's passage. Use the supplied journal
guidelines or a sample from the author to judge terminology, register and
emphasis. Read the passage for its role in this particular paper. An introduction
can lead from what earlier work explains to the question that remains. A methods
paragraph gives readers the details they need to understand how a result was
obtained. A discussion interprets that result within the limits of the design.
In a theoretical or humanities paper, the thread may run from a definition or
quoted passage through the author's reading of it to the argument it supports.

## Follow the technical argument

Read across sentence boundaries. Can a reader tell which quantity was measured,
what it was compared with and how the comparison bears on the claim? Put each
unit, denominator, baseline or condition next to the result it qualifies. Use
the same name for the same concept. Distinctions such as mean and median, or
reported confidence and demonstrated ability, carry part of the argument.

Keep the technical term and simplify the words around it. “We labelled the
samples using…” gives the method a clearer place in the sentence than “We
performed the labelling of the samples using…”. In either language, give each
value a clear point of comparison. Once the sentence identifies the starting
and ending conditions, a second introductory comparison can usually go.
In the interpretation, check the grammar as well as the statistics. Each
intervention can contribute to a change in the outcome. Make clear which
contribution the study can estimate.

For example, suppose the cache and index both changed during a trial. A results
paragraph that also explains the limit of the comparison might read as follows.

> During the trial, the cache hit rate rose from 60% to 80%, and median query latency fell from 50 to 35 ms. Because the index was also updated during the trial, this comparison cannot isolate the effect of caching on latency.

> 试验期间，缓存命中率从 60% 升至 80%，查询延迟的中位数从 50 毫秒降至 35 毫秒。由于同期还更新了索引，仅凭这次比较，无法判断延迟降幅中有多少来自缓存调整。

Both versions give the measurements before interpreting them. The second
sentence names the other change and explains why it matters for attribution.
State an observed result plainly. Use qualification where the reasoning needs it.

After moving a sentence, check which claim each citation supports. A reference
for a method belongs with the method even when the next sentence reports a new
finding. Compare numbers, quotations, terms and citations with the source.
Where the text leaves a material gap, flag the specific question for the author.

## Editing assessed work

For assessed work, coach the student on the reasoning they have already supplied.
Show a targeted language edit and explain its effect on the reader. If a claim
needs evidence or the logic is incomplete, point to the exact passage and ask
the student to make the substantive decision. When a light polish is all that
is requested and permitted, edit the existing wording and keep the reasoning
in the student's hands.
